SECTION 1: EXPENSE CLAIMS
--------------------------

1.1 Eligible Expenses
Business-related expenses incurred by employees in the course of their duties are eligible for reimbursement.
Eligible categories include: business travel, client entertainment, professional development, office supplies, and communication costs.
Personal expenses, fines, penalties, and non-business entertainment are strictly non-reimbursable.

1.2 Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the Finance Portal (SAP Concur) within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
Each claim requires: original receipt or digital copy, expense category, business justification, and project/cost center code.
Claims submitted after 30 days require VP-level approval and may be subject to delayed processing.
Incomplete claims will be returned to the employee for correction — resubmission resets the processing timeline.

1.3 Approval Workflow
Claims under $500: Approved by direct manager.
Claims $500–$2,000: Approved by department head.
Claims over $2,000: Requires VP approval and Finance review.
Claims over $10,000: Requires CFO approval with detailed business case documentation.
All approvers must process claims within 5 business days of submission.

1.4 Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit to the employee's registered bank account.
International reimbursements may take up to 15 business days due to cross-border processing.
Employees may not use personal credit cards for purchases exceeding $5,000 — a corporate card or purchase order must be used.


SECTION 2: BUDGET APPROVALS
-----------------------------

2.1 Annual Budget Cycle
The annual budget planning cycle begins in October and concludes with board approval in December.
Department heads submit budget proposals using the Budget Template available on the Finance Portal.
Budget proposals must include: personnel costs, operational expenses, capital expenditures, and contingency (minimum 5%).

2.2 Budget Modification
Mid-year budget modifications require a Change Request Form with justification and impact analysis.
Modifications under 10% of departmental budget: Approved by VP.
Modifications over 10%: Require CFO approval and Finance Committee review.
Emergency budget allocations can be fast-tracked through the CFO's office with a 48-hour turnaround.

2.3 Cost Center Management
Every expense must be tagged to a valid cost center and project code.
Creating new cost centers requires Finance team approval and VP authorization.
Quarterly cost center reviews are conducted by Finance — inactive cost centers are archived after 6 months.


SECTION 3: TRAVEL ALLOWANCES
------------------------------

3.1 Domestic Travel
Economy class airfare for flights under 4 hours; premium economy for flights over 4 hours.
Hotel accommodations: Up to $200/night in standard cities, up to $350/night in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, DC).
Daily meal allowance: $75 per day (breakfast $15, lunch $25, dinner $35). Receipts required for individual meals over $50.
Ground transportation: Ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) or rental car with prior approval. Mileage reimbursement: $0.67/mile for personal vehicle use.

3.2 International Travel
All international travel requires pre-approval from the VP and the Travel Desk at least 14 days in advance.
Business class is permitted for flights exceeding 8 hours.
International per diem rates follow the U.S. Department of State published rates for the destination country.
Travel insurance is mandatory and provided by the company. Employees must register their trip with Corporate Security.

3.3 Travel Booking
All bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal (Concur Travel) to ensure policy compliance.
Direct bookings are only permitted when the corporate portal does not service the destination.
Loyalty program points earned on business travel belong to the employee but must not influence booking decisions.
