===============================================
    TECHCORP — NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
           Effective: January 1, 2025
           Version: 3.0 | Internal Use
===============================================

SECTION 1: FIRST DAY CHECKLIST
---------------------------------

1.1 Before You Arrive
Your manager will send a Welcome Email 3 days before your start date with your schedule and point of contact.
Complete all pre-boarding paperwork in Workday (tax forms, emergency contacts, direct deposit).
Download and install the following apps on your personal phone: Microsoft Authenticator, Slack, and Okta Verify.

1.2 Day One Schedule
09:00 — Welcome session with HR: badge collection, office tour, and security briefing.
10:00 — IT setup: Collect your laptop, configure email, VPN, and security tools.
11:00 — Meet your team: Introduction session with your manager and immediate team members.
12:00 — Team lunch (company-sponsored).
13:30 — Compliance training: Code of Conduct, Data Privacy, and IT Security (mandatory, online modules).
15:00 — Meet your buddy/mentor: Your assigned onboarding buddy will guide you through the first 90 days.
16:00 — Self-guided setup: Explore internal tools, join relevant Slack channels, and set up your development environment.

1.3 Required Day-One Tasks
Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Employee Handbook acknowledgment.
Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all company accounts.
Complete the mandatory compliance training modules (must be finished by end of Week 1).
Update your profile in the Employee Directory with your photo, title, and contact information.


SECTION 2: SYSTEM ACCESS REQUESTS
-------------------------------------

2.1 Standard Access
On your first day, IT will provision the following standard access: email (Outlook), Slack, Confluence, JIRA, VPN, and the Employee Portal.
Additional tool access (GitHub, AWS, database access, etc.) must be requested through the IT Service Desk on ServiceNow.
Access requests require manager approval and are processed within 2 business days.

2.2 Elevated Access
Access to production systems, customer data, or admin tools requires a Privileged Access Request (PAR).
PARs require approval from: your manager, the system owner, and the Information Security team.
Elevated access is granted on a time-limited basis (maximum 90 days) and must be renewed.
All privileged access is logged and subject to quarterly access reviews.

2.3 Software Installation
Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices. The approved software catalog is available on the IT Portal.
Requests for non-standard software must include a business justification and IT Security review.
Open-source software usage must comply with the Open Source Policy — certain licenses (GPL, AGPL) require Legal review.


SECTION 3: MENTOR ASSIGNMENT
-------------------------------

3.1 Buddy Program
Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team or a related team.
The buddy serves as an informal point of contact for questions about culture, tools, and day-to-day processes.
Buddy assignments last for 90 days and include weekly 30-minute check-in meetings.

3.2 Formal Mentorship
After the probation period, employees may request a formal mentor through the Mentorship Portal.
Formal mentors are senior employees (L5+) from any department who have volunteered for the program.
Mentorship engagements run for 6 months with monthly 1-hour sessions focused on career development.


SECTION 4: PROBATION PERIOD
------------------------------

4.1 Duration and Structure
The standard probation period is 90 calendar days from the date of joining.
During probation, the employee and manager will have structured check-ins: Week 1, Week 4, Week 8, and the final review at Week 12.
Each check-in includes a written feedback form documenting progress, areas for improvement, and goals for the next period.

4.2 Probation Assessment Criteria
Performance: Meeting the role's core responsibilities and deliverables.
Cultural Fit: Demonstrating alignment with TechCorp values — collaboration, integrity, and innovation.
Learning Agility: Ability to ramp up on tools, processes, and domain knowledge.
Attendance and Reliability: Consistent attendance and punctuality during the probation period.

4.3 Probation Outcomes
Successful completion: The employee transitions to regular full-time status with full benefits.
Extended probation: If performance is borderline, probation may be extended by 30 days (once only) with documented improvement goals.
Unsuccessful probation: Employment may be terminated with 1 week's notice during or at the end of probation.
