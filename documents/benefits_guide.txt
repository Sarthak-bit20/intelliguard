SECTION 1: HEALTH INSURANCE
------------------------------

1.1 Medical Coverage
TechCorp provides comprehensive medical insurance through UnitedHealthcare for all full-time employees and their eligible dependents.
Three plan tiers are available:
  — Platinum PPO: $0 deductible, $10 copay for doctor visits, $500 out-of-pocket maximum. Employee contribution: $120/month.
  — Gold HMO: $250 deductible, $20 copay, $2,000 out-of-pocket maximum. Employee contribution: $60/month.
  — Silver HDHP+HSA: $1,500 deductible, 20% coinsurance after deductible, $4,000 out-of-pocket maximum. Employee contribution: $25/month. Company contributes $1,000/year to HSA.
The company covers 85% of the premium for individual coverage and 70% for dependent coverage across all plans.
Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one with no waiting period.

1.2 Dental and Vision
Dental insurance (Delta Dental): Covers preventive care at 100%, basic procedures at 80%, and major procedures at 50%. Annual maximum: $2,500.
Vision insurance (VSP): Covers annual eye exam, $200 frame allowance, and contact lens benefit. Employee contribution: $8/month.

1.3 Open Enrollment
Open enrollment period: November 1–30 each year. Changes take effect January 1.
Life events (marriage, birth, adoption, divorce) allow mid-year plan changes within 30 days of the event.
New employees are enrolled automatically in the Gold HMO plan and may change during their first 30 days.


SECTION 2: PROVIDENT FUND / RETIREMENT
-----------------------------------------

2.1 401(k) Retirement Plan
All employees are eligible from day one of employment.
The company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of base salary, and 50% of contributions from 4%–6%.
Maximum effective company match: 5% of base salary.
Vesting schedule: Employee contributions are always 100% vested. Company match vests at 33% per year over 3 years.
Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,500 for 2025; $31,000 for employees aged 50+).
Investment options include: S&P 500 index fund, total market fund, bond fund, target-date funds, and a self-directed brokerage window.

2.2 Financial Wellness
TechCorp provides free access to a financial advisor through Fidelity for retirement planning and investment guidance.
Quarterly financial wellness webinars cover topics such as tax optimization, estate planning, and debt management.
Emergency hardship withdrawals from 401(k) are permitted under IRS guidelines with HR approval.


SECTION 3: STOCK OPTIONS & EQUITY
-------------------------------------

3.1 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
Eligible employees may purchase TechCorp stock at a 15% discount through bi-annual enrollment periods (January and July).
Maximum contribution: 15% of base salary or $25,000/year (whichever is less).
Shares are purchased at the end of each 6-month offering period at the lower of the enrollment date or purchase date price, minus 15%.

3.2 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)
RSU grants are provided to eligible employees at hire and during annual refresh cycles.
Standard vesting: 4-year schedule with 25% cliff vesting at year 1 and quarterly vesting thereafter.
RSU values are based on fair market value at vesting date and are taxed as ordinary income.
Refresh grants are based on performance rating, level, and retention considerations.

3.3 Stock Option Exercise
Employees with stock options must exercise within 90 days of termination (voluntary or involuntary).
Early exercise is permitted for ISO options with an 83(b) election filed within 30 days.
The Equity Administration team (equity@techcorp.com) provides exercise support and tax guidance.


SECTION 4: ANNUAL BONUS
--------------------------

4.1 Bonus Structure
Annual bonuses are paid in March for the prior fiscal year's performance.
Target bonus percentages by level:
  — Individual Contributor (L1–L3): 5–10% of base salary.
  — Senior IC / Team Lead (L4–L5): 10–15% of base salary.
  — Manager / Director (L6–L7): 15–25% of base salary.
  — VP and above (L8+): 25–40% of base salary.

4.2 Bonus Calculation
Bonus = Base Salary × Target Bonus % × Individual Performance Multiplier × Company Performance Multiplier.
Individual Performance Multiplier: Based on annual review rating (0.0x for rating 1, up to 1.5x for rating 5).
Company Performance Multiplier: Based on company revenue and profitability targets (ranges from 0.5x to 1.25x).
Employees must be employed on the bonus payment date to be eligible. Pro-rated for employees who joined mid-year.

4.3 Spot Bonuses
Managers may award spot bonuses of $500–$2,000 for exceptional contributions outside normal responsibilities.
Spot bonuses require VP approval and are processed through the HR Portal within 2 pay cycles.
Spot bonuses are limited to 4 per employee per fiscal year.
