SECTION 1: CODE OF CONDUCT
----------------------------

1.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct.
Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect, dignity, and courtesy.
Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind will not be tolerated.
This includes but is not limited to discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

1.2 Workplace Harassment Policy
IntelliGuard Corp maintains a zero-tolerance policy for workplace harassment.
Harassment includes: unwelcome verbal or physical conduct, sexual harassment, hostile work environment creation, and cyberbullying.
Employees experiencing or witnessing harassment must report it immediately to HR or use the anonymous Ethics Hotline.
All complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially. Substantiated claims result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

1.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family relationships that may create a conflict of interest.
Outside employment or consulting work must be disclosed to and approved by the employee's VP.
Employees may not use company resources, proprietary information, or their position for personal gain.
Gifts or entertainment from vendors/clients exceeding $50 in value must be reported to the Compliance team.

1.4 Confidentiality
Employees are bound by the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) signed at the time of hiring.
Company proprietary information, trade secrets, and client data must not be disclosed to unauthorized parties.
This obligation continues after the termination of employment.
Social media posts about company business require prior approval from the Communications team.

1.5 Substance Abuse Policy
The use, possession, or distribution of illegal substances on company premises is prohibited.
Alcohol consumption is permitted only at company-sanctioned events and must be moderate.
Employees suspected of impairment during work hours may be subject to testing and disciplinary action.
The company offers a confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for substance abuse support.


SECTION 2: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
------------------------------

2.1 Health Insurance
The company provides comprehensive health insurance for all full-time employees and their dependents.
Coverage includes: medical, dental, and vision.
Plans offered: PPO (Premium), HMO (Standard), and HDHP with HSA (High Deductible).
The company covers 85% of the premium for individual coverage and 70% for family coverage.
Open enrollment period: November 1–30 each year. Life event changes (marriage, birth) allow mid-year enrollment.

2.2 Retirement Benefits (401k)
Employees are eligible to participate in the company 401(k) plan from their first day of employment.
The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 6% of their base salary.
Vesting schedule: Immediate vesting for employee contributions; company match vests over 3 years (33% per year).
Employees may choose from a range of investment options including index funds, target-date funds, and bond funds.

2.3 Life and Disability Insurance
Basic life insurance: 2x annual salary, fully paid by the company.
Supplemental life insurance: Available at group rates, up to 5x annual salary.
Short-term disability: 60% of base salary for up to 26 weeks.
Long-term disability: 50% of base salary after 26 weeks, for the duration of the disability or until age 65.

2.4 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
Eligible employees may purchase company stock at a 15% discount through payroll deductions.
Enrollment periods: January and July of each year.
Maximum contribution: 15% of base salary or $25,000 per year (whichever is less).
Holding period: Shares must be held for 1 year after purchase for favorable tax treatment.

2.5 Professional Development
The company provides an annual learning and development budget of $3,000 per employee.
Approved expenses include: conferences, online courses, certifications, books, and training programs.
Tuition reimbursement: Up to $10,000 per year for degree programs related to the employee's role.
Employees must maintain a grade of B or equivalent to receive tuition reimbursement.

2.6 Wellness Program
Annual gym membership reimbursement: Up to $600 per year.
Mental health support: 12 free counseling sessions per year through the EAP.
Wellness days: 2 additional paid days off per year for wellness activities.
On-site amenities: Meditation rooms, standing desks, and healthy snack stations at all office locations.

2.7 Other Benefits
Commuter benefits: Pre-tax transit and parking deductions up to IRS limits.
Employee referral bonus: $5,000 for successful referral hires (paid after 90-day probation).
Parental support: Subsidized backup childcare (up to 20 days/year) and on-site lactation rooms.
Volunteer time off: 2 paid days per year for community service activities.


SECTION 3: OFFICE HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
----------------------------------------

3.1 Standard Work Hours
Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
Core hours (mandatory availability): 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.
Flexible scheduling is available — employees may adjust start/end times with manager approval.
Total expected weekly hours: 40 hours for full-time employees.

3.2 Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours exceeding 40 per week.
Overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's manager.
Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime but may receive compensatory time off at the manager's discretion.
Consistently working excessive hours (50+ per week) will trigger a workload review by HR.

3.3 Time Tracking
All employees must log their work hours in the Time Tracking system (Workday) on a daily basis.
Entries must be submitted by end of day Friday each week.
Failure to submit timesheets for 2 consecutive weeks may result in a payroll hold.
Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving team timesheets by Monday noon.

3.4 Attendance and Punctuality
Regular attendance and punctuality are essential for team collaboration and productivity.
Employees who will be late or absent must notify their manager before 9:30 AM.
Chronic tardiness (more than 3 instances per month) will be addressed through the progressive discipline process.
Unexcused absences of 3 or more consecutive days without notification may be treated as voluntary resignation.

3.5 Breaks
Employees are entitled to two 15-minute paid breaks per day in addition to their lunch break.
Break times should be coordinated with team members to ensure adequate coverage.
Smoking breaks must be taken in designated outdoor areas only.

3.6 Remote Work Transition
Employees transitioning between office and remote work must update their work location in Workday.
Equipment pickup/return must be coordinated with the IT department at least 3 business days in advance.
All remote work arrangements are subject to the WFH policy outlined in the HR Policy Manual.
International remote work requires Legal and Tax team approval due to jurisdictional implications.
