SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
------------------------

1.1 Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)
All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at a rate of 2 days per month starting from the date of joining.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year.
Any leave beyond the carry-forward limit will lapse on December 31st without compensation.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a licensed physician.
Chronic illness accommodations can be requested through HR with supporting medical documentation.
Mental health days are included under sick leave — employees are encouraged to use them without stigma.

1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the first two children; 12 weeks for subsequent children.
Paternity leave: 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
Adoption leave follows the same structure as maternity/paternity leave.
Surrogacy leave: Commissioning parents are eligible for 12 weeks of paid leave.

1.4 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.
Immediate family includes: spouse, children, parents, siblings, grandparents, and in-laws.
Additional unpaid leave of up to 5 days may be granted at the manager's discretion.

1.5 Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year as per the regional holiday calendar.
Employees required to work on public holidays will receive compensatory time off or 2x pay.

1.6 Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least 5 business days in advance.
Emergency leave can be communicated via email or phone to the immediate supervisor.
Leave approval is at the discretion of the reporting manager, considering team workload.
Unapproved absence for more than 3 consecutive days may result in disciplinary action.


SECTION 2: WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY
----------------------------------------

2.1 Eligibility
All employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) are eligible for WFH.
Certain roles requiring physical presence (e.g., facilities, lab technicians) are exempt.
New hires must work from the office for the first 90 days unless otherwise approved by their VP.

2.2 WFH Schedule
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week under the hybrid model.
Core collaboration days are Tuesday and Thursday — all employees must be in-office on these days.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and must be reviewed quarterly.

2.3 WFH Requirements
Employees must maintain a stable internet connection with minimum 25 Mbps download speed.
A dedicated, quiet workspace is required — working from cafes or public spaces is discouraged for security reasons.
Company-issued equipment (laptop, headset, monitor) must be used for all work activities.
Employees must be available during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone.

2.4 WFH Communication
Employees must update their status on Slack/Teams during work hours.
Video must be enabled for all scheduled meetings unless bandwidth constraints exist.
Response time for messages during core hours should not exceed 30 minutes.

2.5 WFH Expense Reimbursement
Employees on fully remote arrangements receive a monthly stipend of $150 for internet and utilities.
One-time ergonomic setup allowance of $500 is available for home office equipment.
Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of purchase.


SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS
---------------------------------------

3.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually: Mid-Year (July) and Year-End (January).
The review process includes self-assessment, peer feedback (360-degree), and manager evaluation.
Calibration sessions are held at the department level to ensure fair and consistent ratings.

3.2 Rating Scale
Ratings follow a 5-point scale:
  1 — Needs Improvement: Consistently below expectations; requires a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
  2 — Partially Meets Expectations: Some areas need development; specific goals will be set.
  3 — Meets Expectations: Solid, reliable performance; meets all role requirements.
  4 — Exceeds Expectations: Frequently delivers above and beyond; demonstrates leadership qualities.
  5 — Exceptional: Consistently outstanding; considered for accelerated promotion or special recognition.

3.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees rated 1 or 2 for two consecutive cycles will be placed on a 90-day PIP.
The PIP includes specific, measurable goals, weekly check-ins with the manager, and HR oversight.
Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in role reassignment, demotion, or termination.

3.4 Promotions and Compensation
Promotion eligibility requires a minimum rating of 4 in the most recent review cycle.
Salary adjustments are based on performance rating, market benchmarking, and budget allocation.
Annual merit increases range from 3% (Meets Expectations) to 15% (Exceptional).
Stock option grants are available for employees rated 4 or above in senior roles (L5+).

3.5 Continuous Feedback
Managers are expected to provide ongoing, documented feedback — not just during review cycles.
Monthly 1:1 meetings are mandatory and should include career development discussions.
Employees can request ad-hoc feedback sessions at any time through the HR portal.

3.6 Appeals Process
Employees who disagree with their review rating may file an appeal within 15 business days.
Appeals are reviewed by the skip-level manager and an HR Business Partner.
The appeals committee decision is final and binding.
