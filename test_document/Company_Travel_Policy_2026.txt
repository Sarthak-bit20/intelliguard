Global Travel and Expense Policy (Updated May 2026)

1. General Guidelines
All employees must book travel through the approved corporate portal. Flights over 6 hours are eligible for Premium Economy. Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy.

2. Meal Allowances
The daily meal per diem is $75 USD for domestic travel and $120 USD for international travel. Alcohol cannot be expensed under any circumstances.

3. Ride-Sharing and Cabs
Employees are encouraged to use public transit where available. For corporate ride-sharing, use the approved corporate account. Tipping up to 20% is reimbursable.
